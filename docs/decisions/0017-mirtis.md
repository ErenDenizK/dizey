# 0017: Mırtis, an orange tabby, replaces Birim

- Status: accepted (Eren picked the design after nine rounds)
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Supersedes: [0015](0015-birim.md) (Birim, rejected)

## Context

Birim, a unit square whose moods were 2x2 matrices, was rejected as soulless and out of proportion. Eren reviewed
cat designs over nine rounds and approved Mırtis. The design was built and tested as a plain-JS rig outside the app
(node tree per level of detail, one update path, storyboards, a technical bar T1 to T8). This decision ports it as is.

## Decision

- `src/character/mirtis.ts` is a typed port of the rig and its motion. Geometry, colours, moods and springs are
  unchanged. The rig has one declarative node tree per (LOD, head bust); `createCat` builds the DOM once and `update`
  sets only attributes whose value changed; parts that appear (open mouth, tongue, heart, brows, toes) fade, so no node
  is created or removed in motion.
- Colours are `--mirtis-*` tokens in `tokens.css` (the same in every palette; the palette generator leaves them
  alone) applied by classes in `mirtis.css` (`mt-f-*` fill, `mt-s-*` stroke), because SVG presentation attributes
  cannot take `var()`.
- `Mirtis.tsx` keeps Birim's API (`size`, `mood`, `wake`, `className`, `ref.react(mood)`). Moods map onto storyboards:
  happy is `correct`, oops is `wrong`, wow, think, rest, sleep (`sleepy`), lesson end is `proud`, `wake` plays
  `wake`, and the profile avatar (a real button with a Turkish aria-label) plays `purr`.
- Size picks the rig (see Presence): verdict (56 px) and dock (72 px) the full body, profile (112 px) the full-detail
  bust, hero the full body. No cat is under 48 px except the app icon.
- Motion runs on the spring engine's clock: frames are an engine ticker that exists only while the cat moves; blink
  waits are plain timers, so an idle cat keeps `engine.isActive()` false. `engine.reducedMotion` is followed frame by
  frame: reactions get stiffer and half as long, never cut.
- The lesson end's `--glow` is written by `shine.ts` on the svg itself; the engine sets the custom property and CSS
  turns it into a drop shadow, as before.
- App icons are the rest-mood bust on a tile in the `--bg` colour, generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs` from the same
  rig and tokens (`pnpm icons`).

## Consequences

- `mirtis.test.ts` guards the design's promises without a browser: same writes in every frame, at most 8 units per
  60 fps frame (over 4 only as 25% of an element's travel), opacity steps at most 0.25, settled within 1.2 s, no frame
  while idle, deterministic per seed, each with and without reduced motion.
- One measured exception, pinned in the test: the small bust under reduced motion in `proud` moves an eye 4.3 units
  in a frame (limit 4). The design's browser check never ran the small LOD. It is under 2 px at 40 px and the rig was
  not retuned.
- Birim's code, tests and `--birim-*` tokens are gone. The Satır and Sütun duo (`Duo.tsx`, `rig.ts`) stays because
  the character lab still uses it.
- Mırtis on Yol rests (blinking), not in the closed-eyes grin, so she looks awake while you choose.

## Presence

How the cat is shown across the app, after Eren found her too small and hard to see on some screens.

1. **One cat, one place, a real job.** At most one Mırtis on screen, and never on a question while it is being solved.
2. **She perches.** Her paws rest on the top edge of the card she belongs to and her body rises above it. She never
   floats and never sits beside a tile.
3. **She enters and leaves through that edge.** `Perch` (`src/character/Perch.tsx`) clips everything below the edge line
   and moves her on an engine spring (`land`, a small overshoot) from behind the card; leaving she sinks behind it
   (`leave`). No fade. Under reduced motion the rise is the shorter effect spring, never a cut (PR-16).
4. **Sizes.** No cat under 48 px except the app icon, and the full body wherever there is room.
   - Yol: on the dock card, top right, 72 px (the tile no longer carries her). The wake storyboard plays there.
   - Lesson verdict: 56 px, on the verdict sheet's top edge at the right; she rises just after the dock's entrance
     (`VERDICT_PERCH_DELAY`), so it reads as one rise.
   - Sen: the avatar is 112 px (88 px under 700 px of height). Lesson end: the hero is `clamp(128px, 30vh, 176px)`, and
     `clamp(96px, 16vh, 128px)` while a rozet shows; the change glides on the engine (`--badge` on the stage).
5. **Between pages** each page's cat enters with its page and leaves with it. No cross-page flights.

Guards: `e2e/mirtis.spec.ts` (perch position, one cat per screen, every cat at least 48 px wide).

## Moments

Until now the learner saw three moods in a lesson (rest, correct, wrong). Moments give the others a job without ever
disturbing the solving. `src/character/moments.ts` (`pickMoment`) is pure and deterministic: it gets the step, the
first-try run, whether the last question was missed, the wrong count, solved or not, the last step that showed a moment
and whether halfway has had its turn, and returns `{ play, line }` or null. No randomness; a line with variants is
picked by step index.

1. Priority, highest first:
   - **Streak:** the first-try run reaches 3, then every 3. `wow`, "N üst üste!".
   - **Comeback:** solved first try right after a missed question, or solved on a later try after a wrong. `proud`.
   - **Two wrongs** and the answer revealed (a gentle `think`, "Birlikte bakalım."; never blaming).
   - **Halfway:** the first verdict at or past 50 % of the steps, once per lesson (`correct` if clean, else `proud`).
   - **First step:** the lesson's first explain step; `wake`, then glad. "Hadi başlayalım."
2. **Cap:** one moment per 3 steps, except a streak. The greeting counts. A plain verdict keeps `correct` / `wrong`
   with no bubble. The halfway moment is spent by the first verdict past 50 %, shown or not.
3. **Never while solving:** moments only appear on a verdict or on an explain step, never on an unanswered question.
4. **Bubble** (`Bubble.tsx`, `Cheer.tsx`): one line of at most 28 characters (`strings.mirtis`), left of the perched
   cat's head with its tail pointing at her, at most 55 vw wide. It rides the perch's lift, so it enters after the rise
   (opacity and scale on the engine) and sinks behind the card's edge with her. It is `aria-hidden`; the verdict's
   `aria-label` carries the line, so it is announced once.
5. The first step's cat perches on the top edge of the first card, over the top bar, so the step counter fades out
   (`useEase`) while she is there. No new sounds.
