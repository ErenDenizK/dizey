## Which of our methods worked

| Method | Evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence-level tracker with owner, status and evidence per item | 23:28 "half skipped" feeling came from work spread over threads with no per-sentence view; the tracker found most items done but not merged (5 PRs queued), 2 real misses (type size, title), and closed all 59 | Keep, make standard for every multi-item prompt |
| Measure against a reference before building | old vs new (42 px vs 19 px, matrix area 63%), original lessons (six rules) | Keep, make standard |
| Self-critique walks of the live build at 375 and 390 px after merges | 15 findings (9.1 to 9.15) fixed and re-verified overnight | Keep |
| Turn each fixed defect class into a guard | `lesson-review.mjs` caught #63; `type-space.test.ts`, `conformance.test.ts`, `agreement.test.ts`, `motion.test.ts`, `tokens.test.ts` back every "it's all fine" claim in tracker section 10 | Keep |
| Show, don't ask; ship each slice live with "what to test" | complaints dropped from 5 per block to 2; morning verdict | Keep |
| Parallel threads per workstream | 35 PRs in 7.5 h | Keep, but with one owner per shared surface (added after #74/#75) and no version bumps on feature branches |
| Rules derived from general research | day-1 shell judged "no nizam"; lessons.md 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31 rows | Stop as a starting point; use research to fix gaps found against a reference |
| Absolute rules from one statement ("nothing old") | would have discarded what Eren valued most | Already covered by PR-03 |
| Interpreting a word through the thread's specialty | "font" became scale consistency; Eren meant bigger and fuller (8.12) | Fix: restate each item as the visible change Eren will see |
| Verifying on a few still screenshots or unsettled frames | #63 regression; 9.10 judged from an early frame | Fix: full walk, settled frames |

## Root causes

1. **Good results came from closed loops on concrete targets.** Every big win had a reference or a symptom (a target), a method to find gaps (director, frame review, measurement), a guard so it stays fixed, and a visible check on the live app. Block A had none of the four; block C had all four.
2. **Bad results came from open loops.** Abstract taste words, research-derived rules and verbal questions have no observable target, so neither side could tell if the result met the ask until Eren saw it hours later.
3. **"Skipped details" was mostly a visibility gap, partly an interpretation gap.** Most items were done but not live; the real misses happened where a word was read through a thread's specialty instead of as the visible change Eren described.
4. **Parallel speed costs coordination.** Shared lines (`package.json` version) and shared surfaces (stage layout) collided until each got one owner.
