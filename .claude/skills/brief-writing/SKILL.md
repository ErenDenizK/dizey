# Brief writing

A brief leaves no question unanswered but does not prescribe the solution. Over-specifying the "how" makes the result worse.

## Include

- **Goal** and Eren's moment of use (e.g. studying on his iPhone between classes).
- **Relevant decisions and principles** by ID, with one line each. Not the whole board.
- **Hard constraints:** language policy, "nothing from the old project", git rules, anything Eren made firm.
- **Sources:** exact files, doc sections and links.
- **Done condition and evidence** (tests, screenshots, recomputation, report format).
- **Output destinations:** Turkish report for Eren; English decision or research notes for the repo; new ledger items sent to the system thread.

## Leave out

- Chat history and old discussions.
- Step-by-step instructions for how to do the work; say "choose the best approach" instead.
- Anything from the old project except raw course material.

## Check before sending

Could the receiver start without asking a question? Is anything in the brief longer than the spec it points to? If yes, cut.
