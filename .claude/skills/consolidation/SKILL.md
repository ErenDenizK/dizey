# Consolidation

Keep the system small and current. This is housekeeping, not ceremony: finish in one short pass.

## Steps

1. **Ledger.** Tick boxes that are live and checked, then move ticked boxes out of the Çalışma listesi (the P sections keep the history). Every open box has an owner. Merge standing rules that say the same thing.
2. **Board.** Keep only current state. Move stale detail to decision records or the ledger. The board should read in two minutes.
3. **Principles.** Review the ones whose review point has come; resolve contradictions (ask Eren when needed); retire the unused or harmful with a reason.
4. **Lessons.** Look for repeated root causes in `docs/lessons.md`; turn repeats into principle candidates or fix the relevant skill.
5. **Memory.** Delete wrong or outdated memory files; keep the index short and pointing to files.
6. **Report.** Send Eren one short Turkish note: added, changed, retired. No recap of unchanged things.

Only the system thread runs consolidation.
